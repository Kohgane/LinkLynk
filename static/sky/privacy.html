SameSky — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

The short version

Your photos never leave your device. SameSky extracts three color values from your sky photo locally, and only those three colors — plus your device's timezone name and the hour of day — are sent to our server to power the "Today, under the same sky" strip and Sky Twin matching.

What we collect

  • Three hex color codes per catch (e.g. #7fa8d9)
  • Your IANA timezone name (e.g. Europe/Lisbon) — used as a coarse city label
  • Hour of day (0–23)

What we do NOT collect

  • No photos or images
  • No GPS or precise location
  • No name, email, phone number, or account
  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking SDKs

Storage

Your photos and calendar live only in your device's local storage. Color records on our server are anonymous and cannot be linked back to you.

Deletion

Clearing the app's site data removes everything on your device. Server-side color records contain no identifiers; contact us for any request.
